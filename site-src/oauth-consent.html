OVERWORLD WORKSPACE

Connect your workspace

Sign in with the Google account that owns your Overworld workspace. Gemini will be able to read your projects and, when you ask, add projects, add tasks, or update project details.

Gemini is requesting:
  • Read projects and tasks
  • Add projects and tasks
  • Update status, finish lines, and notes

Gemini may ask you to confirm an action before it changes your map. You can disconnect the app from Gemini’s Connected Apps settings.
